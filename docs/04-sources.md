# 04 — Sources (what the app and the coach are built from)

## Curriculum and standards
- QSI academic model (essential units, TSW outcomes, A/B/P): https://brindisi.qsi.org/academics and any QSI school's
  /academics page; QSI curriculum overview: https://www.qsi.org/academic-program/curriculum
- Common Core State Standards, Grade 4 and 5 Mathematics and ELA (official text): search "corestandards.org grade 5" —
  the ELA and Math standards pages. These are the codes used in data/.
- NGSS Grade 4 and 5 performance expectations: https://www.nextgenscience.org (search "5-PS1", "5-LS2" etc.)
- Houghton Mifflin *Go Math!* Grade 5 chapter list (chapter 1 = Place Value, Multiplication and Expressions) — the QSI
  Mathematics-10 units are keyed to it.

## MAP Growth
- What RIT means and learning statements per band: https://teach.mapnwea.org/impl/maphelp/Content/AboutMAP/WhatRITMeans.htm
- Learning Continuum (school login required): https://teach.mapnwea.org/impl/maphelp/Content/Data/SampleReports/LearningContinuumRef.htm
- 2020 norms overview: https://www.nwea.org/uploads/MAP-Growth-Normative-Data-Overview.pdf ; full tables:
  https://teach.mapnwea.org/impl/NormsTables.pdf
- MAP Growth → Khan Academy crosswalk PDFs (K–2 verified at https://cdn.nwea.org/docs/MAP+Growth+Grades+K-2+to+Khan+Academy.pdf ;
  the Grades 3–5 sibling document exists — search "MAP Growth Grades 3-5 to Khan Academy" and save it to data/).
- Khan Academy MAP-linked practice evidence (+26–38% vs projection at 30+ min/week):
  https://blog.khanacademy.org/learning-paths-informed-by-map-growth
- Default MAP test windows (fall 15 Aug–30 Nov): https://developers.nwea.org/faqs/what-are-default-term-window-and-test-window-dates-map-growth

## Item banks and style models (free, with rubrics)
- Smarter Balanced sample items (grade/subject/target filters): https://sampleitems.smarterbalanced.org ;
  practice tests: https://smarterbalanced.org/our-system/students-and-families/samples/
- NAEP Questions Tool — released items with rubrics and student-response examples: https://www.nationsreportcard.gov/reading/sample-questions/
  (also math and science)
- CommonLit (free grade-levelled passages with standards-aligned questions): https://www.commonlit.org — link out only.
- ReadWorks (free passages, Lexile levels): https://www.readworks.org — link out only. [not fetched in this research; well known]
- Project Gutenberg for public-domain passages: https://www.gutenberg.org
- Khan Academy Grade 4/5 math and grammar exercises (free): https://www.khanacademy.org — link out for extra practice.

## Teaching and tutoring research (the coach's rulebook)
- Rosenshine, B. (2012) *Principles of Instruction*, American Educator (AFT) — daily review, small steps, questions,
  models, guided practice, checks, high success rate, scaffolds, independent practice, weekly/monthly review.
  Summaries: https://www.innerdrive.co.uk/blog/cognitive-science-rosenshine/ ;
  https://my.chartered.college/early-career-hub/applying-rosenshine-to-primary-practice/
- Bloom (1968) mastery learning (80–90% then move on); Dunlosky et al. (2013) on retrieval and spacing;
  Karpicke & Roediger (2008); Rohrer & Taylor (2007) interleaving.
- Retrieval practice in elementary children: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4786565/
- Lepper & Woolverton (2002) expert tutors: Socratic, reflective, progressive, indirect, encouraging.
- LLM tutoring principles (correctness, scaffold not give-away, self-correction, no overload): MathTutorBench
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.18940 ; MathDial https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.14536 ; effective vs ineffective tutor moves
  (no "praise without action", no answer give-away — Tutor CoPilot): https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.09994
- Learning-principles AI tutor case study (retrieval difficulty must be moderate): https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.13060
