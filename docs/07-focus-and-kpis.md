# 07 — Knowing what he actually did: focus, effort, KPIs

## 1. Why not camera and microphone recording
The parent offered camera, microphone and screen access. Recommendation: **no continuous recording or AI watching**.
- It does not measure learning; it measures posture. The app already has a better signal: what he answers, how fast,
  and whether the window is in front of him.
- A model cannot watch a live video feed reliably or cheaply; clips of a child's face and room would be sent to a
  third-party API for analysis, which is the wrong trade for a marginal signal.
- Overt surveillance of a 10-year-old predictably damages trust and intrinsic motivation, which are the two things this
  project depends on for nine more months. Monitoring should be about *work done*, visible to him, not about watching him.
- The microphone is used — for the coach — with a push-to-talk button and no recording between turns.
Where blocking matters, use the OS tools that already exist and are reliable: iPad Screen Time / Android Family Link /
Windows Family Safety to allow only the app and the allow-listed sites during the study window.

## 2. What the app measures instead (telemetry, all visible to Elchin too)
- **Active minutes**: time with the tab visible and an answer or coach turn in the last 90 s. Idle > 90 s pauses the clock.
- **Focus ratio** = active minutes / session minutes.
- **Cadence**: answers per minute vs the expected time per item (from tier and format). Very fast + wrong = guessing flag;
  very slow + correct = weak-evidence flag (docs/06 §2).
- **Completion**: planned blocks finished vs planned (daily review, coach block, reading, learn-ahead).
- **Help-seeking**: hints used, "Show me a step" used, retries.
- **Wins**: skills moved up; reviews passed; loops completed.
Optional, parent-controlled: a "session check-in" where he photographs his paper working at the end — feeds the
working-shown rubric, not surveillance.

## 3. KPIs on the parent dashboard (weekly and monthly)
| KPI | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Active minutes vs target | sum of active minutes / (daily target × days) | effort, honestly measured |
| Focus ratio | active / elapsed | tells you if the session is real or drifting |
| Velocity | skills moved to Secure per week | progress rate; drives the projected dates |
| Projected date | days to bring remaining priority-1 skills to Secure at current velocity | early warning |
| Retention rate | share of Mastered skills that pass their FSRS reviews and the monthly Knowledge Check | whether September survives to March |
| Coach cycles per skill | mean explanation cycles before Secure | which skills are hard for him; where the school should help |
| Guessing rate | fast-and-wrong share | tells you when he's rushing |
| Reading minutes and passages | per week | the habit that moves MAP Reading most |
| MAP RIT by instructional area | entered from the school report each window | the external truth |

## 4. How to use them with him (the "future guidance" the parent asked for)
Weekly 10-minute review together, looking at the same dashboard he sees: one KPI that went up, one to work on, one
thing he chooses. Never lead with focus ratio; lead with wins. Rewards are tied to *completed blocks and reviews*, not to
scores.
