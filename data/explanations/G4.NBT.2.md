# Add and subtract multi-digit numbers with regrouping (G4.NBT.2, 4.NBT.B.4)

## The idea (in small steps)
1. Line up the places: ones under ones, tens under tens. Squared paper helps.
2. Add or subtract from the right (ones) to the left.
3. Adding: if a column makes 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry 1 to the next column.
4. Subtracting: if the top digit is smaller, borrow 1 from the next place to the left (it becomes 10 in this column).
5. Borrowing across a zero: you cannot borrow from 0, so borrow from the next non-zero place first; the 0 becomes 9 (10 minus the 1 you passed on).
6. Check subtraction by adding back: answer + the number you took away = the number you started with.

## Worked example
60,304 − 27,856. Ones: 4 − 6 cannot do. The tens digit is 0, so go to hundreds: 3 becomes 2, the tens 0 becomes 10, borrow one to the ones: tens 9, ones 14. 14 − 6 = 8. Tens: 9 − 5 = 4. Hundreds: 2 − 8 cannot do; borrow from thousands (0 → go to ten-thousands: 6 → 5, thousands 0 → 10 → 9, hundreds 12). 12 − 8 = 4. Thousands: 9 − 7 = 2. Ten-thousands: 5 − 2 = 3. Answer 32,448. Check: 32,448 + 27,856 = 60,304.

## Faded example (he completes)
5,003 − 1,247. Ones: 3 − 7 cannot do; borrow across the zeros: thousands 5 → ___, hundreds 0 → ___, tens 0 → ___, ones 3 → ___ . Now subtract each column: ___ . (Answers: 4; 9; 9; 13; 3,756.)

## Anticipated errors (and the scaffold)
- Subtracting the smaller digit from the larger regardless of position (6 − 4 instead of 4 − 6). Scaffold: "Top minus bottom. Can you? If not, borrow."
- Forgetting to reduce the digit you borrowed from. Scaffold: cross out and rewrite the digit before moving on.
- Borrowing from a 0 and leaving it as 0. Scaffold: "You cannot borrow from nothing — go one more place left."
- Misaligned columns. Scaffold: squared paper, commas lined up.

## Representation sequence
1. Words: the column chant — "can I? if not, borrow".
2. Picture: place-value blocks; trading a hundred for ten tens.
3. Table: the column algorithm on squared paper with the crossings-out shown.
4. Story: 60,304 manat in the bank, 27,856 spent.
5. Teach it: he explains borrowing across a zero to a younger child using blocks.

## Check by explaining back
"What do you do when you have to subtract from a zero?"
