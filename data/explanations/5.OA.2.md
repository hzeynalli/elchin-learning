# Write and interpret numerical expressions from words without evaluating (5.OA.2, 5.OA.A.2)

## The idea (in small steps)
1. The words tell you what happens first. Whatever happens first goes inside brackets if something else is done to the result.
2. "The sum of A and B" = A + B. "The difference between A and B" = A − B. "The product of A and B" = A × B. "Twice / three times" = × 2 / × 3.
3. "…, then multiply by C" means the whole first part is multiplied: (A + B) × C.
4. Read your expression back into words to check it says the same thing.
5. Do not calculate — the task is to write the instructions, not the answer.

## Worked example
"The sum of 12 and 8, multiplied by 3." First thing: the sum, 12 + 8. Then the whole sum is multiplied by 3, so brackets: (12 + 8) × 3. Read back: "twelve plus eight, all times three." Matches.

## Faded example (he completes)
"Subtract 6 from 40, then multiply by 5." First: 40 − ___ . Then times 5 on the whole thing: ( ___ ) × 5. (Answer: (40 − 6) × 5.)

## Anticipated errors (and the scaffold)
- Writing the numbers in the order heard: "subtract 6 from 40" as 6 − 40. Scaffold: "Which number do you start with? Which one is taken away?"
- Leaving out the brackets: 12 + 8 × 3. Scaffold: "Is only the 8 multiplied, or the whole sum?"
- Calculating instead of writing the expression. Scaffold: "Freeze — write the recipe, not the cake."
- Interpreting "3 × (18,932 + 921)" by computing it. Scaffold: "How does it compare with 18,932 + 921 without working it out?"

## Representation sequence
1. Words ↔ symbols: a two-column card, words on the left, expression on the right.
2. Picture: draw the bracket as a box; the operation outside acts on the whole box.
3. Table: key phrases (sum, difference, product, twice, half) and their symbols.
4. Story: "Aysel has 12 stickers and 8 more; her three friends each get the same amount" → (12 + 8) × 3 vs 12 + 8 × 3.
5. Teach it: he writes a words-puzzle for the coach to turn into an expression, then checks it.

## Check by explaining back
Ask him to explain, without numbers, when brackets are needed.
